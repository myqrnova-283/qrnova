What is a UPI QR Code?

A UPI QR code stores payment details that a compatible payment app can read after scanning. QRNova creates a QR code using your UPI ID, optional payee name and optional amount.

How to create a UPI QR code

  1. Enter your UPI ID exactly as provided by your bank or payment app.
  2. Optionally add the payee name and amount.
  3. Tap Generate UPI QR Code.
  4. Scan and test it using a trusted UPI app before printing or sharing.

Where can you use a UPI QR code?

  • Small shops and local businesses
  • Freelance services and invoices
  • Donation desks and community events
  • Printed bills, menus and counters

Important payment safety

QRNova does not process payments or verify UPI IDs. Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Always confirm the recipient name and amount inside your own UPI app before paying.
