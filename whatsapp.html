What is a WhatsApp QR Code?

A WhatsApp QR code lets people open a WhatsApp chat quickly by scanning a QR code. QRNova creates a QR code from your WhatsApp phone number and optional pre-filled message.

How to create a WhatsApp QR code

  1. Enter your WhatsApp number with country code, without spaces or the plus sign.
  2. Add an optional message.
  3. Tap Generate WhatsApp QR Code.
  4. Download the PNG and share or print it.

Where can you use it?

  • Business cards and shop counters
  • Product packaging and flyers
  • Social media profiles and posters
  • Customer support and business enquiries
  • Events and promotional materials
